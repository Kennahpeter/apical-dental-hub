About us

A calmer kind of dental care.

A neighbourhood clinic on Mwiki Road, Kasarani.

Apical Dental Hub was started with a simple idea: good dental care should feel easy to reach. We opened in Sunton, Kasarani, so families nearby could see a dentist close to home, any day of the week.

Today we offer everything from routine check-ups to braces and root canals, with clear prices and a free first consultation. We take our time, explain your options, and help you leave smiling.

What we believe.

Kindness first

Every patient is treated with patience and respect.

Honest advice

We recommend only what you need, and explain why.

Safe and clean

Strict sterilisation and modern, well-kept equipment.
